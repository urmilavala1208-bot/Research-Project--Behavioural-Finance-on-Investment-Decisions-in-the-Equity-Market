# Research Project--Behavioural Finance on Investment Decisions in the Equity Market
“Impact of Individual Equity Investor’s Behavioural Biases on Investment Decisions in the Equity Market – Surat Region.”
Key takeaway: Investor decisions are influenced not only by financial information but also by psychological and behavioural factors. The study highlights the importance of awareness and education to reduce the impact of behavioural biases on investment decisions.
